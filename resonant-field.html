Resonant Field

A field begins in order.
Lines align, structures hold.

A small disturbance enters.
Oscillations spread.
The system starts to breathe.

Driven by spring-like forces and rhythmic waves,
the grid bends, ripples, and slowly destabilizes.

What appears stable is never fixed—
only waiting to be moved.
