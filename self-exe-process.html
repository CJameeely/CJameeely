Process

Based on the story board, I planned to make an interactive page with TouchDesigner where testers can see a huge amount of facial features on the screen, including themselves, and they can try to find their own features by open and close their hands to control the screen.

So firstly I started to collect random facial features. Then I built a draft TouchDesigner page.

The collected elements include: eyes, ears, nose, mouth.
