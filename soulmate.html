Soulmate

We're too quick to treat personality as a parameter to optimize: extroverts crave quiet, introverts crave noise—as if flipping a switch could make life easier.

Soulmate is about a pill like that. You swallow it, and your mode of living gets "switched." But once you actually move into someone else's rhythm, you realize the discomfort isn't a side effect—it's a signal. A reminder that you don't have to become anyone's template.

This switch feels like an experiment, and also like a detour. What remains isn't an answer of "more extroverted" or "more introverted," but a more honest question: What kind of life truly fits me?
