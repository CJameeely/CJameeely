Language Distortion

Responsive installation · Robotic plants

Language Distortion translates facial expression, voice, and posture into plant-like movement, exploring how understanding can happen before or beyond words.

Interaction · Three plants, three channels

Each plant reads one channel of the visitor and ignores the words. The flower opens and closes with facial expression, the succulent inflates and collapses with the emotion in the voice, and the willow bends with posture.

01 · Mechanical flower

02 · Succulent

03 · Willow branch

Technique · Emotion as position, not label

Face landmarks, body pose and voice are analysed live in the browser and placed in a continuous valence–arousal space instead of being sorted into words. Three Arduinos receive that reading over serial and turn it into servo, air-pump and motor movement.
